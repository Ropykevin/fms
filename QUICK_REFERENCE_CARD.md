## Quick Data Entry Steps

### Add New Animal
1. Click "Add Livestock"
2. Select Species (Cow, Goat, Sheep, etc.)
3. Enter Breed (e.g., Holstein, Nubian)
4. Select Gender (Male/Female)
5. Enter Date of Birth
6. Enter Weight (positive number)
7. Enter Ear Tag (or leave empty for auto-generation)
8. Add Notes (optional)
9. Click "Add Livestock"

### Add Feeding Record
1. Click "Add Feeding Record"
2. Select Animal from dropdown
3. Enter Feeding Date
4. Enter Feed Type (Hay, Grain, Pellets)
5. Enter Quantity (positive number)
6. Add Remarks (optional)
7. Click "Add Feeding Record"

### Add Medical Report
1. Click "Add Medical Report"
2. Select Animal from dropdown
3. Enter Report Date
4. Enter Diagnosis
5. Enter Treatment
6. Enter Medicine
7. Enter Cost (non-negative)
8. Enter Vet Name
9. Add Remarks (optional)
10. Click "Add Medical Report"

### Add Produce Record
1. Click "Add Produce Record"
2. Select Animal from dropdown
3. Enter Report Date
4. Enter Produce Type (Milk, Eggs, Wool)
5. Enter Quantity (positive number)
6. Add Remarks (optional)
7. Click "Add Produce Record"

## Validation Rules
- ✅ Weight: Must be positive
- ✅ Quantity: Must be positive
- ✅ Cost: Must be non-negative (can be zero)
- ✅ Dates: Use date picker
- ✅ Ear Tags: Auto-generated if empty

## Tips
